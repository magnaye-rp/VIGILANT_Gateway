Detection Strictness
Choose a preset or select Custom to tune every parameter yourself
How detection works: The system tracks social media requests only (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, etc.) — DNS, iCloud, and other background traffic are ignored. It needs 30 social requests before making any decision, so opening an app normally won't trigger anything. Once warmed up, two things can flag you: an RPM spike (request rate jumps above your average) or sustained scrolling (on social media for X minutes straight).
