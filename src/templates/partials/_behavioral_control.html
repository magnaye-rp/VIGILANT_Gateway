How it works: The system tracks how long you actively scroll social media. After each threshold, bandwidth drops progressively. Two ways to release: (1) Close the app or stop scrolling for the idle reset period — if activity drops below ~3 reqs/min, the throttle auto-releases. (2) Make zero social requests for the idle reset period.
